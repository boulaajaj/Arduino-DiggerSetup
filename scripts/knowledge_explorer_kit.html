How to use the map

Click a nodeSelect it, show its details and light up what it connects to. Collapsed groups open.
Double-clickCollapse or expand a module or cluster. On empty space: fit the view.
Drag a nodeIts children travel with it and linked nodes follow on springs. It stays pinned where you drop it.
Right-click a nodeUnpin it (or pin it).
Drag empty space · wheelPan · zoom toward the cursor.
HoverPreview a node and its neighbors.
/Search topics, repository paths, thresholds, routes, and telemetry fields.
P or "Trace path"Then click or search a second node: the shortest chain of real links between them lights up.
MSwitch between Organic and Mind map layout.
E C F U HExpand all · collapse · fit · unpin all · hide the side controls.
ZFull screen: the map covers the whole monitor, taskbar included, so no panel is cut off. Z or Esc leaves it.
EscClear the selection, path or search.
Branch linksSolid lines show containment. Hover or select a leaf to show its direct links to other visible leaves.
